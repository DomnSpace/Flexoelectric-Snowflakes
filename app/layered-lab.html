DOMNSPACE / PHYSICS EXPERIMENT · MODEL B

FIVE-LAYER ICE GROWTH

Five independent ice-occupation fields compete for the same vertically diffusing vapor. These are computed mass fractions, not five repeated render sheets.

← Snowflake Observatory
This is an experimental coarse-voxel model. Exposed ice interfaces consume vapor from one shared reservoir; the grown ice partially replaces local gas volume. Latent heat now enters an optional conservative gas/ice heat-conduction solver, which changes local equilibrium vapor concentration on subsequent steps. Physical layer thickness is 0.75 µm, not a molecular ice layer.

Deposited masses are derived from the actual gas→ice face transfers. No center-layer weighting is imposed.

Heat transport is a coarse fixed-property voxel approximation, not calibrated ice-facet thermodynamics or natural convection. The model is presently validated only on small domains. Internal ribs, 3D optical effects and calibrated edge-SDAK are not yet predicted.
