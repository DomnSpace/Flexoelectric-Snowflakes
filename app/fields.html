Interpretation

Vapor lines show where diffusion supplies mass. Heat lines show where latent heat escapes. χ lines combine both by comparing gas vapor density with the locally warmed ice-equilibrium density. A tip can have high vapor supply but weak χ if latent heating cancels it.
